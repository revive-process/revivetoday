Enterprise initiatives

Every initiative changeshow work executes.Few of them can see it.

AI, process, risk, transformation, customer experience and cash are funded separately and measured separately. They share one dependency: the way work actually runs across the systems, people and automation underneath.

What an initiative plans for

  • The target process and its scope
  • The business case and its benefits
  • A new system, control or model

What execution decides

  • The paths the work actually takes
  • Where time and effort accumulate
  • What people do around the change

Initiatives are planned against the designed process. They succeed or fail against the executed one.

The common blind spot

Work crosses more systemsthan any one system can see.

An order, a case, a claim or a request leaves execution evidence across the systems, people and automation that handle it. Each system records its part. RE-ViVE connects that evidence to reconstruct the paths the work actually took — where rework, waiting, handoffs and exceptions accumulate. That same execution evidence sits underneath every initiative on this page.

One observed workflow · six different questions

The same execution evidence. Six different readings.

Account Opening was one of the bank's largest and most critical onboarding services. Across 13,685 requests, the observed workflow averaged 16.62 days from submission to completion — 10.14 days of rework and 6.48 days of work done once. The same evidence reads differently depending on the initiative being funded.
