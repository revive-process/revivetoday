Why processes are hard to see

Enterprises are not short of process data. They are short of a way to read it as a process.

  • The record shows the outcome

    A closed order, a paid invoice, a completed request. A single record rarely explains the complete route the work took to get there, or how often it moved backward across systems and teams.

  • Work crosses more systems than any one system can see

    ERP, CRM, workflow engines, portals, spreadsheets and email each record part of the execution. RE-ViVE connects that evidence so the end-to-end path can be reconstructed across the systems, people and automation involved.

  • Averages conceal the cost

    A mean cycle time describes the clean majority and hides the small population of cases that consume most of the effort and produce most of the escalations.

  • Documentation describes intent

    Process maps, SOPs and workshop outputs record how the work is meant to run. Local workarounds become standard practice without ever reaching the diagram.

One method, every process

Each process asks a different business question, but RE-ViVE uses the same method underneath: reconstruct execution from operational evidence, then expose paths, waiting, rework, handoffs and variants. Choose the workflow closest to the problem you need to understand.

How it works, on every process

No process remodeling. No new workflow instrumentation. Read-only source access.

  1. Step 1

    Choose a process and provide read-only extracts from the systems that already record it.

  2. Step 2

    We reconstruct execution

    RE-ViVE maps source records into its Execution Data Model, allowing each order, case or request to be reconstructed across the systems involved.
