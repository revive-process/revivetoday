About RE-ViVE

We built RE-ViVE to solve a problemwe kept seeing in how businesses operate.

Every business depends on processes and workflows to get work done. Orders are fulfilled. Customers are onboarded. Requests are approved. Products are made. Payments are processed.

But that work rarely happens in one place. It moves across applications, databases, workflows, automation and people. Each may capture its own part, while the complete execution remains difficult to see.

RE-ViVE was built to make that execution visible.

Using the operational data businesses already generate, RE-ViVE reconstructs how work actually executes—helping organizations see where work slows down, repeats, deviates and can be improved.

Why RE-ViVE Exists

The problem became clear before the product did.

Businesses have invested for decades in applications, databases, integration, reporting and business intelligence. These systems provide enormous value. They tell us what happened, measure performance and help run individual parts of the business.

But an important question remained surprisingly difficult to answer.

How did the work itself actually execute from beginning to end?

Answering that question became the foundation for RE-ViVE.

RE-ViVE Today

The home of Execution Intelligence.

RE-ViVE gives organizations a continuous understanding of how work actually executes across people, systems, automation and AI.

With read-only access to underlying business data, RE-ViVE reconstructs individual orders, cases, requests and other units of work across the systems involved—without requiring organizations to first remodel their processes or build event logs for us.

Process agnostic

Follow execution across the systems and teams involved, regardless of the business process.

Built from execution evidence

Understand what actually occurred rather than only what a process was designed to do.

Designed to scale

Move from one execution to patterns across thousands or millions of executions.

What Guides Us

A simple belief guides what we build.

Organizations already generate extraordinary amounts of evidence about how their work executes. The challenge is turning that fragmented evidence into understanding.

We believe businesses should be able to see that execution clearly—before they redesign a process, automate it, apply AI to it, or decide where to invest.
