Diagnose · Contextualize · Evaluate

Once you understand execution, what do you do with it?

Diagnose

See what is actually happening.

Where is execution breaking down or behaving differently?

Identify delays, rework, variants, exceptions and instability before deciding what to change.

Contextualize

Understand why it is happening.

What conditions are associated with those execution patterns?

Connect execution behavior to customers, products, policies, systems, dependencies and objectives.

Evaluate

Know whether it worked.

Did the action produce the intended result?

Compare execution before and after transformation, automation or AI-driven change.

DCE is flexible. Diagnose, Contextualize and Evaluate can be used independently or together. What you learn through evaluation can sharpen the next diagnosis and context.
