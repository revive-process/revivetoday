THE OPERATING LANDSCAPE BEFORE RE-ViVE

The problem was not a lack of data. It was a lack of usable execution context.

The bank needed to understand nearly 100 service workflows and reduce SLA exposure, but the execution evidence was fragmented across Excel files, workflow platforms and custom applications.

1,800+ distinct activitiesSystems logged thousands of activity types, but documentation did not reliably identify which activities belonged to which workflow.
Workflow knowledge kept changingAd-hoc customer needs, regulatory requirements and operating changes altered execution month to month.
No clean blueprint to start fromTeams understood parts of the work, but no current end-to-end model existed for nearly 100 workflows.
Near-real-time visibility matteredThe bank wanted to understand what had happened within a roughly four-hour operational window, not only through retrospective analysis.
