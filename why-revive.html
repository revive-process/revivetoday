RE-ViVE’s execution-level question

What happened to this loan, this payment, this customer, and why?

The first question is answered with a picture. The second needs evidence. Banks are judged on the second: by customers, by examiners, and by their own boards. RE-ViVE is built to answer it.

The RE-ViVE design philosophy

Don't infer the process.
Reconstruct the execution.

RE-ViVE deterministically reconstructs execution from operational events and their relationships, rather than starting with an assumed process model and trying to fit behavior around it.

The process map is the result of that reconstruction — not the assumption that drives it.

That difference makes individual paths, independent activities, exceptions, changing structures and source-level evidence central to the analysis, not afterthoughts.

A different starting point

The maps look alike.
The foundations don't.

Process mining offers many discovery and modeling techniques. RE-ViVE takes a different starting point: operational events and their relationships are used to reconstruct each observed execution deterministically. The resulting maps can be analyzed without relying on a predefined ideal process or a statistically inferred representative path.

Why this is hard to copy

Four persistent challenges.
One different starting point.

Researchers continue to improve how process analysis represents concurrency, multidimensional context and evolving relationships. RE-ViVE addresses these needs through its deterministic execution foundation, rather than requiring the same discovery-and-abstraction assumptions.

01 / CONCURRENCY

Parallel work is not rework.

A directly-follows graph can suggest loops when independent activities finish in different orders. RE-ViVE identifies same-level activities and distinguishes parallel work from actual repetition.

For example: sanctions screening and ID verification can finish in either order without either check being repeated.
02 / CONTEXT

30–100 dimensions. One execution foundation.

RE-ViVE implementations support dozens of process-context dimensions, not just filters on time or amount. Teams can examine relationships across product, channel, branch, policy, risk and other business perspectives without separately rebuilding the underlying execution for each view.

For example: Product, channel, branch, risk tier, relationship manager, policy version: same data, any view.
03 / CHANGE

When the workflow changes, the view does too.

RE-ViVE captures changing relationships and reconstructs the execution of each period as observed. New activities, retired steps and altered routes can be compared over time without forcing them into a fixed process template.

For example: A new fraud check added in Q2 shows up in Q2, not as noise across the year.
04 / AI AGENTS

Agents tied to business outcomes.

The same reconstruction extends to AI agents, tool calls and traces. Where business keys and telemetry exist, agent behaviour links to the loans, cases and payments it touched.

For example: An extraction agent's retries traced to the disputes that missed their deadline.
One foundation · multiple perspectives

The evidence stays the same. The question changes.

Parallel activities are recognized as peers, context dimensions expose different populations, and period comparisons reveal new or retired steps. None requires inventing a representative execution.

Built for scrutiny

When the examiner asks,
open the record.

Banking teams have traced individual transactions and requests surfaced by RE-ViVE back to their underlying operational systems to verify what actually happened.

Whether the record originates in loan origination, core banking, payment processing, or a case-management platform, the investigation can move from the execution insight to the relevant source evidence.
