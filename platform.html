02 A different implementation approach

Spend less time preparing the process. More time improving it.

Traditional process-mining implementations typically involve several steps before analysis begins: define the process scope, prepare the event structure, configure and validate the analysis, then build the measures and views the business needs. RE-ViVE consolidates much of that work into one reusable execution foundation built from the source data.

Connect the source data, establish the RE-ViVE execution foundation, then spend your time analyzing and improving the process.

  • Every variant survives

    Common paths, rare paths and exceptions remain available for analysis.

  • No modeling backlog

    No separate manually maintained process model for every new execution view.

  • Process agnostic

    Use the same RE-ViVE approach across different workflows and business processes.

  • Hours of your time, not weeks

    Your team helps us understand the data and the process. RE-ViVE does the heavy lifting.

03 One process, four ways to use it

Connect once. Use it four ways.

RE-ViVE connects to the source records behind a process and shows how the work actually moved from start to finish. That same execution intelligence is then available across all four ViVE modules.

The same reconstructed process powers every module. You do not rebuild it separately for analysis, compliance, reporting and AI.

04 The Execution Data Model

What RE-ViVE knows about every execution

Once RE-ViVE reconstructs the process, it keeps both the full path and the business context around it. You can see the process at a high level and still drill all the way down to an individual order, case or request.

variants

Every path the work took

See common routes, rare routes and exceptions — including how often each occurred and how long each took.

rework

Where work repeated or went backward

See loops, retries and repeated activities, along with the time they added.

bottlenecks

Where time was lost

Find the activities, queues and handoffs where work waited or slowed down.

durations

How long each step and case took

Measure time at the case, path, activity and transition level — not just one overall average.

handoffs

Where work moved

See transfers between teams, roles, systems or locations and the delays around them.

resources

Who or what performed the work

People, teams, systems, automation and AI can all be analyzed as part of the same process.

business context

The business details around the process

Analyze by product, customer, region, value, channel, priority and other mapped source attributes.

individual executions

The complete path for one case

Drill from an aggregate view to the actual sequence for a single order, claim, request or other unit of work.

05 Depth, volume and breadth

Built for large, complex processes

Some processes are relatively simple. Others contain hundreds of activities, millions of executions, thousands of paths and events spread across many systems. RE-ViVE was built to handle that complexity without forcing the process into a simplified view before you can analyze it.

  • Depth

    Hundreds of activities and deeply nested execution paths can remain available for analysis.

  • Volume

    Analyze from one execution to millions of cases and very large event populations.

  • Variation

    Thousands of observed paths can be compared without making low-frequency exceptions disappear from the underlying data.

  • Business context

    Slice the same process by product, region, customer, team, value, channel and other mapped attributes.

Complex processes stay complex underneath. RE-ViVE gives you a way to understand them without throwing away the detail that matters.

07 Ten days

From source access to a first execution view in about 10 business days

A typical first implementation focuses on one process and the source data behind it. Timing depends on source access and data complexity, but the first execution view is typically available in about 10 business days.

DAYS 1–2

Connect & understand the data

Establish read-only access and understand the business keys, activities and source relationships involved.

DAYS 3–5

Build the execution model

Map the relevant records into RE-ViVE and establish the process view with its business context.

DAYS 6–8

Analyze the process

Review paths, timing, rework, bottlenecks, handoffs and other patterns in the observed execution.

DAYS 9–10

Review findings with your team

Walk through what RE-ViVE found and agree where deeper analysis or improvement should begin.
