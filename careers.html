Careers at RE-ViVE

Work on a problemevery large enterprise has.

Orders, cases and requests move across dozens of systems before they are finished. Almost no organization can see how that actually happened. That is the problem we work on.

We build Execution Intelligence on the operational data of real enterprises.

You will work with real enterprise execution data, not demo sets.

Our work starts from the systems businesses actually run on, which means the problems are concrete, the data is messy, and the results are visible to the people who own the process.

Why This Work

Close to the systems that run the business.

Most software teams are one or two steps removed from how an enterprise really operates. RE-ViVE sits directly on the execution evidence: the orders, approvals, handoffs, exceptions and reworks recorded across ERP, CRM, workflow engines and custom applications.

That proximity changes the work. Reconstructing how a single order travelled through six systems is a harder and more interesting problem than it first appears.

Can you make messy enterprise data tell the truth about how work ran?

That question sits behind almost every role here.

How We Work

Clear ownership, real problems.

Work is organized around processes and customers rather than layers of hierarchy, so the person building something usually talks directly to the person who needs it.

Because we work with production data under read-only access, care and judgement matter as much as speed.

Ownership over scope

Roles here are defined by the problem you are responsible for, not by a narrow function.

Close to the customer

Engineering, delivery and go-to-market work on the same processes rather than in separate silos.

Evidence over opinion

The same principle we apply to customer processes applies internally: decisions follow what actually happened.
